# Support

- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue on [php-io-extensions/plutovg](https://github.com/php-io-extensions/plutovg/issues). Include `php --ri plutovg`, your OS and CPU, PHP version and build (NTS or ZTS), and the smallest script that shows the problem.
- **How a PlutoVG call behaves:** the comments in [`lib/plutovg/include/plutovg.h`](lib/plutovg/include/plutovg.h) and PlutoVG's sources are the reference; this extension adds only what the README's translation section describes.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.
